PEPList

● live Visit project →

Have you ever wanted to make a playlist of episodes from podcasts? PEPList lets you do that and have the whole thing accessible as if it were its own podcast. You can subscribe to it in your favorite podcast app.

How It Works

  1. Create a playlist: give it a title, description, and pick a cover image;
  2. Add episodes: search for a podcast by name or paste a feed URL, then pick the episodes you want; and,
  3. Subscribe: copy the PEPList feed URL into any podcast app (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Pocket Casts, etc.) and your playlist shows up like any other podcast

That’s it. PEPList generates a standard RSS feed, so it works everywhere podcasts work.

What You Can Do With It

Curate across shows: Pull episodes from different podcasts into one feed. Put together a playlist of your favorite interviews across ten different shows, or collect every episode about a specific topic.

Organize and reorder: Drag and drop episodes into the order you want. Add notes to remember why you picked an episode.

Share: Every playlist has a public page you can share with a link, and an embed code if you want to put it on a website.

How it works

All podcasts are lists of audio files. PEPList references those files with attribution as they exist on their original podcasts. It does not copy or alter any episodes.

Feedback

PEPList is a work in progress. If you have feedback, suggestions, or run into issues, send an email to attnfeed@gmail.com.
